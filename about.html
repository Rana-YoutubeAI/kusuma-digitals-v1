About Rana

The Person Behind Kusuma Digital Solutions

I started Kusuma Digital Solutions after watching good St. Louis businesses lose customers to competitors with better websites — not better products, just better online presence. That felt wrong. And fixable.

I've spent years building technical tools and frameworks for the private companies and created websites, digital tools, and systems for businesses What I found is that most agencies are built for large clients. Small businesses get handed to junior staff, locked into long contracts, and left reading reports nobody can explain.

Kusuma Digital Solutions is built to be the opposite of that. It's just me — the same person who picks up your call, builds your site, and fixes it if something goes wrong. No handoffs. No surprises.

What working with me actually looks like

  • You deal directly with me — start to finish, no account managers in between
  • Fixed quotes before work begins — no open-ended billing or surprise invoices
  • Monthly updates in plain English — what happened, what's next, what it means
  • I'm still here after launch — your site stays my responsibility
  • One person who knows your whole setup — website, SEO, hosting, all of it
