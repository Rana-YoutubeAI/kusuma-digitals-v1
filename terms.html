Kusuma Digital Solutions provides the following services to small businesses, primarily in the St. Louis metropolitan area:

  • Website design and development
  • Local SEO and Google presence management
  • Monthly website care and maintenance plans
  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization
  • Custom tools and automation
  • Full-stack development (hourly or project-based)

The specific deliverables for your project are defined in a written quote or email confirmation. If it's not in that document, it's not in scope.

2. Project Scope & Changes

Work begins based on the agreed scope. If you request additions or changes that fall outside the original agreement, we'll provide a separate quote before proceeding. We won't do work we haven't agreed on — and we won't charge you for anything we haven't quoted.

Scope changes must be confirmed in writing (email is fine). Verbal agreements don't count.

3. Payment

Website projects (one-time)

  • 50% deposit is required before work begins. This secures your spot in the schedule.
  • 50% balance is due on project delivery, before site launch.
  • We don't launch until the balance is paid.

Monthly services (SEO, care plans)

  • Billed monthly, in advance, at the start of each billing cycle.
  • First month is due before work begins.

Hourly work

  • Invoiced weekly, payable within 7 days.
  • Rate is $85/hour unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Late payments

Invoices unpaid after 15 days may result in a pause on all active work until the balance is settled. We'll send a reminder before pausing — we'd rather keep working than chase invoices.

All prices are in US dollars.
